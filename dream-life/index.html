Your objects

These feed the Vehicles, Animals, Workshop, Kitchen, Clothes and Gear lines. Tick objects one by one. Prices shown are for ; typing a price sets it for every country. Co. puts an object under the company; Pro % is its real business share.

What the table can't show

Example numbers are realistic September 2026 estimates for a couple in their 20s in a rural outdoor region, in euros (SEK 11.0 and NOK 11.7 per euro). Confirmed 2026 rules: French 18.6% social levies on investment gains and 31.4% flat tax; Swedish ISK tax 1.065% above SEK 300,000 per person; Finnish capital tax 30%/34% and €100,000 equity savings account; Norwegian wealth tax 1.0% above NOK 1.9 million per person; health cost caps; Swedish property fee cap SEK 10,425; Swedish Hilux vehicle tax. Other values (food, insurance, energy, passes, prices) are best estimates, so edit them when you know better.
