The big picture

1My real results

Once a year, in January. Fill in either the return % or the account value on 31 December. The tool works out the other one. Use the real euro numbers from your statement.

  1. Added = all the money you put into the investment account that year.
  2. Taken out = all the money you took out that year (for example for the van).
  3. Return % = in Interactive Brokers, open PortfolioAnalyst and read the “time-weighted return” for the year. Or type the value on 31 Dec from your year-end statement.
  4. Inflation % is optional: how much prices really rose that year (Eurostat HICP or Statistics Finland). Leave it empty to use the plan’s guess.

Backup and reset

Your plan is saved with this page. For extra safety, keep a backup once a year after you add your results.

This is a planning tool, not financial advice. Future returns are guesses based on long-term forecasts from large asset managers (stocks roughly 5–7.5% a year in euros, bonds about 3%). Random futures use “fat tails”, so big crashes show up about as often as in real markets. Tax rules change and depend on where you live when you sell: check with Vero (Finland), SPF Finances (Belgium) or the NTA (Japan) before big moves.
