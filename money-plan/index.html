Aya & Max Money Plan

One investment account, two separate pots. Bucket 1 safely grows the money for the van and the house. Bucket 2 grows for retirement and lowers its risk step by step. Each January you type in the real results, and the forecast starts again from them. Euro amounts in the plan are in today’s money.

1My real results

Once a year, in January. For each bucket, fill in either the return % or its value on 31 December. Use the real euro numbers from your statement.

  1. Added = money you put into that bucket during the year.
  2. Out = money you took out of that bucket (for example to pay for the van).
  3. Return % = time-weighted return from Interactive Brokers PortfolioAnalyst. Or add up the value of that bucket’s funds on 31 Dec.
  4. Inflation % is optional: how much prices really rose that year (Eurostat HICP or Statistics Finland).

Backup and reset

Your plan is saved with this page. For extra safety, keep a backup once a year after you add your results.

This is a planning tool, not financial advice. Future returns are guesses based on long-term forecasts from large asset managers (stocks roughly 5–7.5% a year in euros, bonds about 3%, cash about the ECB deposit rate). Random futures use “fat tails”, so big crashes show up about as often as in real markets. Tax rules change and depend on where you live when you sell: check with Vero (Finland), SPF Finances (Belgium) or the NTA (Japan) before big moves.
